1. SYSTEM OVERVIEW
________________________________________________________________________________

EduLab is a complete Learning Management System (LMS) that connects four types
of users:

  - STUDENTS      : Browse, enroll in, and complete courses
  - INSTRUCTORS   : Create courses, upload lessons, create quizzes and assignments
  - ORGANIZATIONS : Corporate clients who manage teams and training
  - ADMINISTRATORS: Full control over the entire platform

The system is built with Laravel 13.8 (PHP 8.3+), Tailwind CSS v4, Alpine.js,
and Remixicon. It supports online payments via Paystack and offline payments
via mobile money (Airtel / MTN).

Key URLs:
  Homepage      : https://your-domain.com
  Login         : https://your-domain.com/login
  Register      : https://your-domain.com/register
  Student Panel : https://your-domain.com/dashboard
  Instructor    : https://your-domain.com/instructor
  Organization  : https://your-domain.com/org
  Admin Panel   : https://your-domain.com/admin
  Course Catalog: https://your-domain.com/courses

2. GETTING STARTED - ACCOUNT REGISTRATION
________________________________________________________________________________

Anyone can create an account at /register. The registration page has THREE
tabs at the top: Student, Instructor, and Organization. Click the tab that
matches your role.

------------------------------------------------------------
2A. REGISTERING AS A STUDENT (Default Tab)
------------------------------------------------------------

  REQUIRED fields (marked with *):
    * First Name       - Your legal or display first name
    * Last Name        - Your legal or display last name
    * Email Address    - Must be unique (no existing account with this email)
    * Phone Number     - Any valid phone number
    * Password         - Minimum 8 characters
    * Confirm Password - Must match the password exactly

  OPTIONAL fields: None. All student fields are required.

  What happens after registration:
    1. Your account is created instantly with "active" status
    2. Your email is auto-verified (no email confirmation link needed)
    3. You are automatically logged in
    4. You are redirected to your Student Dashboard at /dashboard

  You can immediately start browsing and enrolling in courses.

------------------------------------------------------------
2B. REGISTERING AS AN INSTRUCTOR
------------------------------------------------------------

  Click the "Instructor" tab on the registration page.

  REQUIRED fields (marked with *):
    * First Name       - Your legal or display first name
    * Last Name        - Your legal or display last name
    * Email Address    - Must be unique
    * Phone Number     - Any valid phone number
    * Password         - Minimum 8 characters
    * Confirm Password - Must match the password exactly
    * Designation      - Your professional title (e.g. "Web Developer",
                         "Data Scientist", "Mathematics Professor")

  OPTIONAL fields: None. All instructor fields are required.

  What happens after registration:
    1. Your account is created with "pending approval" status
    2. ALL admins and staff members receive a notification:
       "New Instructor Registration - [Your Name] ([email]) has registered
        as an instructor and is pending your approval."
    3. You are automatically logged in
    4. You are redirected to the "Pending Approval" page
    5. You CANNOT access instructor features until an admin approves you

  IMPORTANT: You MUST wait for admin approval before you can create courses.
  You will see a "Pending Approval" message with a yellow warning icon.
  Once approved, you will receive a notification: "Instructor Account
  Approved" and can access the full instructor dashboard.

  ALTERNATIVE: You can also register as an instructor from the homepage
  by clicking "Become an Instructor". This form only requires:
    * First Name, Last Name, Email, Password, Confirm Password
    * Phone (optional), Designation (optional), About/Bio (optional)
  This creates the same type of account with pending approval status.

------------------------------------------------------------
2C. REGISTERING AS AN ORGANIZATION
------------------------------------------------------------

  Click the "Organization" tab on the registration page.

  REQUIRED fields (marked with *):
    * Organization Name - Your company/school/institution name
    * Email Address     - Must be unique
    * Phone Number      - Any valid phone number
    * Password          - Minimum 8 characters
    * Confirm Password  - Must match the password exactly

  OPTIONAL fields:
    - Address           - Your physical or mailing address

  NOTE: Organizations do NOT need admin approval. You can access your
  dashboard immediately after registration.

  What happens after registration:
    1. Your account is created with "active" status
    2. You are automatically logged in
    3. You are redirected to your Organization Dashboard at /org
    4. You can immediately create courses and manage instructors

3. GETTING STARTED - LOGGING IN
________________________________________________________________________________

Visit /login to access the login page.

------------------------------------------------------------
3A. STUDENT / INSTRUCTOR / ORGANIZATION LOGIN
------------------------------------------------------------

  The login page has four tabs: Student, Instructor, Organization, Admin.
  Click the tab that matches your role.

  For Student, Instructor, or Organization:
    1. Select your role tab (Student, Instructor, or Organization)
    2. Enter your registered email address
    3. Enter your password
    4. Optionally check "Remember Me" to stay logged in
    5. Click "Login"

  IMPORTANT ROLE CHECK: If you try to log in under the wrong tab, you will
  get an error: "This account is registered as Student, not Instructor.
  Please select the correct tab." You MUST select the correct role tab.

  After login you are redirected to your role-specific dashboard:
    Student      -> /dashboard
    Instructor   -> /instructor
    Organization -> /org

  If an instructor is not yet approved, they will be redirected to the
  "Pending Approval" page regardless of which page they try to access.

------------------------------------------------------------
3B. ADMIN LOGIN
------------------------------------------------------------

  Admin login uses a SEPARATE form (not the same as student login):

    1. Click the "Admin" tab on the login page
    2. Enter the admin email address
    3. Enter the admin password
    4. Click "Login"

  You are redirected to the Admin Dashboard at /admin.

  NOTE: Admin accounts CANNOT self-register. Admin and Staff accounts
  must be created by an existing admin through the Admin Panel.

------------------------------------------------------------
3C. FORGOTTEN PASSWORD
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page
  2. Enter your registered email address
  3. Click "Send Reset Link"
  4. Check your email for the password reset link
  5. Click the link and enter a new password (minimum 8 characters)
  6. Confirm the new password
  7. Click "Reset Password"
  8. You are redirected to the login page to sign in with your new password

4. STUDENT GUIDE - COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH
________________________________________________________________________________

As a student, you have access to a full learning dashboard. Here is everything
you can do, step by step.

------------------------------------------------------------
4A. YOUR DASHBOARD (/dashboard)
------------------------------------------------------------

  When you log in, you see your personalized dashboard with:
  - 5 stat cards: Enrolled Courses, In Progress, Completed, Certificates,
    Wishlist count
  - My Tasks section showing: Pending Assignments, Pending Exams,
    Available Quizzes, Recent Grades
  - Latest Enrolled Courses table with progress indicators
  - Learning Progress circles showing completion percentages

------------------------------------------------------------
4B. BROWSING COURSES (/courses)
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Click "Courses" in the top navigation bar, or visit /courses
  2. Browse the course catalog with filtering options:
     - Filter by Type: Free or Paid
     - Filter by Category: select from available categories
     - Filter by Level: select difficulty level
     - Filter by Tag: select from available tags
  3. Sort courses by: Popular, Newest, Oldest, Price Low-High, Price High-Low
  4. Courses are displayed as cards with thumbnails
  5. HOVER over a course card to see an automatic video preview (if the
     course has a preview video and at least one free preview lesson)
  6. Click on a course card to see the full course detail page

------------------------------------------------------------
4C. VIEWING A COURSE (/courses/{slug})
------------------------------------------------------------

  The course detail page shows:
  - Course title, description, and thumbnail
  - Instructor name and profile
  - Course level and category
  - Number of enrolled students
  - Total lessons, quizzes, and assignments
  - FAQs (top 3)
  - Student reviews with star ratings (approved reviews only)
  - Course announcements (top 5)

  If you are NOT enrolled:
    - You see the course price and an "Enroll Now" or "Add to Cart" button
    - You can toggle the Wishlist button (heart icon)

  If you ARE enrolled:
    - You see a full lesson completion map
    - Quiz and assignment status
    - Certificate status
    - "Continue Learning" or "Start Learning" button

------------------------------------------------------------
4D. ENROLLING IN A FREE COURSE
------------------------------------------------------------

  Method 1 (Direct):
    1. On the course detail page, click "Enroll Now" (for free courses)
    2. You are enrolled immediately
    3. You can start learning right away

  Method 2 (Cart):
    1. Click "Add to Cart" on the course
    2. Go to your cart at /cart
    3. Click "Checkout"
    4. Complete the order (no payment needed for free courses)
    5. You are enrolled immediately

------------------------------------------------------------
4E. PURCHASING A PAID COURSE
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1 - Add to Cart:
    1. On the course detail page, click "Add to Cart"
    2. The course is added to your session cart

  Step 2 - Checkout:
    1. Go to /cart to review your cart
    2. (Optional) Enter a coupon code and click "Apply"
    3. Click "Checkout"

  Step 3 - Payment:
    The checkout page shows your subtotal, any coupon discount, tax, and total.
    Choose one of these payment methods:

    Option A - Paystack (Online Payment):
      1. Click the Paystack button
      2. You are redirected to the Paystack payment page
      3. Enter your card details (card number, expiry, CVV)
      4. Complete the payment
      5. You are redirected back and enrolled automatically

    Option B - Offline Payment (Mobile Money):
      1. Select "Offline Payment" or choose Airtel/MTN
      2. Click "Place Order"
      3. Your enrollment is created with PENDING status
      4. The instructor/admin must approve your payment
      5. You will be notified once approved

  After successful payment:
    - You receive a "Course Enrolled" notification
    - You can access the course immediately (or after approval for offline)
    - The course appears in "My Enrolled Courses" at /dashboard/my-enrolled-course

------------------------------------------------------------
4F. TAKING COURSES - VIEWING LESSONS
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Go to "My Enrolled Courses" at /dashboard/my-enrolled-course
  2. Click on a course, or click "Continue" to resume where you left off
  3. You enter the Course Materials Hub at /courses/{slug}/materials
  4. The hub shows your progress map, all lessons, quizzes, assignments,
     course notes, and certificate status
  5. Click on any lesson to view it
  6. The lesson page has:
     - A video player (if the lesson has a video URL or video file)
     - A document viewer/download (if the lesson has a document)
     - A sidebar showing all lessons with completion checkmarks
     - Your video resume position is saved automatically

  Marking a Lesson Complete:
    - Click the "Mark as Complete" toggle on the lesson page
    - This updates your progress percentage
    - Video position is tracked so you can resume later

  IMPORTANT: You can ONLY view lessons if:
    - You are enrolled in the course, OR
    - The lesson is marked as "Free Preview" (visible without enrollment)

  Lesson Downloads:
    - You can download lesson videos and documents (requires enrollment)

------------------------------------------------------------
4G. TAKING QUIZZES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1 - Find Quizzes:
    - From the course materials hub, click on a quiz
    - Or go to /dashboard/quizzes/my-result to see your results

  Step 2 - Instructions Page:
    - Read the quiz instructions
    - See the time limit, passing score, and attempt limits
    - Click "Start Quiz" when ready

  Step 3 - Take the Quiz:
    - Answer each question
    - Question types include: Multiple Choice, Multiple Select,
      True/False, Short Answer, Essay, Fill in the Blank, Matching,
      and Ordering
    - A timer counts down if the quiz has a time limit
    - If time expires, the quiz auto-submits

  Step 4 - Submit:
    - Click "Submit Quiz" when done
    - Your answers are auto-graded instantly (except essay questions)
    - You see your score, pass/fail status, and individual question results

  NOTE: If the instructor has not released results yet, you will see
  "Awaiting Review" instead of your score. Wait for the instructor to
  release results.

  EXAMS are quizzes marked as "Exam" by the instructor. They appear
  under "Exams" in your sidebar (/dashboard/exams) and follow the
  same process as quizzes.

------------------------------------------------------------
4H. SUBMITTING ASSIGNMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1 - Find Assignments:
    - From the course materials hub, click on an assignment
    - Or go to /dashboard/assignments

  Step 2 - Read Requirements:
    - See the assignment title, description, instructions
    - Note the due date and total marks
    - Check if the instructor has attached an instructions file

  Step 3 - Submit:
    1. Type your answer in the text area (optional if uploading a file)
    2. Upload a file: PDF, DOC, DOCX, or TXT (max 10MB per file)
    3. You can submit text, a file, or both (at least one required)
    4. Click "Submit Assignment"

  IMPORTANT DEADLINE RULES:
    - If the due date has passed and late submissions are NOT allowed,
      your submission will be REJECTED
    - If late submissions ARE allowed, a penalty percentage is applied
      to your score
    - Check the assignment details for deadline information

  After submission:
    - Your submission status shows as "Submitted"
    - The instructor will grade it manually
    - Once graded, you see your score and feedback
    - You receive a notification when graded

------------------------------------------------------------
4I. READING COURSE NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Go to "Course Notes" in the sidebar at /dashboard/course-notes
  2. See all published notes from your enrolled courses
  3. Search by title, summary, or content
  4. Click "View" to read a note
  5. Download attachments if the instructor allows it
  6. Click external links to open additional resources

------------------------------------------------------------
4J. EARNING CERTIFICATES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Certificates are generated AUTOMATICALLY when you complete ALL lessons
  in a course.

  The process:
    1. You mark every lesson as complete in a course
    2. The system detects 100% completion
    3. A certificate is automatically created:
       Title: "Certificate of Completion: [Course Title]"
    4. Your enrollment status changes to "Completed"
    5. You receive a "Course Completed" notification
    6. You can download the certificate as a PDF

  To download your certificate:
    1. Go to "Certificates" in the sidebar at /dashboard/certificate
    2. Click "Download" next to the certificate
    3. A professionally designed PDF certificate is generated with:
       - Your name
       - Course title
       - Date of completion
       - EduLab seal
    4. The file downloads as: certificate-{course-slug}.pdf

------------------------------------------------------------
4K. REVIEWING COURSES
------------------------------------------------------------

  You can ONLY review courses you have COMPLETED.

  1. Go to "Course Review" in the sidebar at /dashboard/course-review
  2. Select a completed course
  3. Rate it from 1 to 5 stars
  4. (Optional) Write a review text
  5. Submit the review
  6. The review requires admin approval before it becomes visible

------------------------------------------------------------
4L. OTHER STUDENT FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Wishlist:
    - Toggle wishlist from any course card or detail page
    - View your wishlisted courses at /dashboard/wishlists

  Support Tickets:
    - Create a ticket at /dashboard/supports/create
    - Select the relevant course (optional)
    - Choose a priority: Low, Medium, or High
    - Write your message
    - View and reply to tickets at /dashboard/supports

  Course Discussions:
    - Visit any enrolled course's discussion page
    - Post questions or replies
    - Instructors and other students can respond

  Notifications:
    - View all notifications at /dashboard/notifications
    - Mark individual or all as read
    - Send notifications to other users at /notifications/send
    - Manage notification preferences at /notifications/preferences

  Profile & Settings:
    - View/edit profile at /dashboard/profile
    - Change password at /dashboard/settings
    - Purchase history at /dashboard/purchase-course

5. INSTRUCTOR GUIDE - COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH
________________________________________________________________________________

As an approved instructor, you have a comprehensive dashboard for creating
and managing courses. Here is everything you can do, step by step.

IMPORTANT: You MUST be approved by an admin before you can access any
instructor features. If you see a "Pending Approval" page, wait for an
admin to approve your account.

------------------------------------------------------------
5A. YOUR DASHBOARD (/instructor)
------------------------------------------------------------

  The instructor dashboard shows:
  - 4 stat cards: Total Students, Total Courses, Total Enrollments,
    Estimated Revenue
  - My Courses table with student counts, prices, and status
  - Quick Actions: Create Course, Manage Quizzes, Manage Assignments,
    View Earnings, My Students, Course Reviews
  - Course Status overview with active/draft/pending counts
  - Recent Activity feed from notifications

------------------------------------------------------------
5B. CREATING A COURSE
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: Click "Add New" or go to /instructor/courses/create

  Step 2: Fill in the course form:

    REQUIRED fields (marked with *):
      * Course Title     - The name of your course
      * Description      - Detailed description of what the course covers
      * Payment Type     - Select "Free" or "Paid"
      * Status           - Select "Draft", "Active", or "Pending"

    If Payment Type is "Paid":
      * Price            - The course price (required for paid courses)

    OPTIONAL fields:
      - Category         - Select from available categories
      - Level            - Select difficulty level
      - Tags             - Check any relevant tags (multiple selection)
      - Learning Outcomes - What students will learn (one per line)
      - Requirements     - Prerequisites or required skills (one per line)
      - Sale Price       - Discounted price (must be less than regular price)
      - Duration         - e.g. "10 hours"
      - Thumbnail        - Course cover image (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, max 5MB)
      - Preview Video    - Course preview video (MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, max 100MB)

  Step 3: Click "Create Course"

  What happens:
    - You are redirected to the course EDIT page
    - A message says: "Course created successfully! Now add lessons."
    - Your course starts in "Draft" status (hidden from students)

  THUMBNAIL UPLOAD:
    - Drag and drop an image, or click to browse
    - Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP
    - Maximum size: 5MB
    - Recommended dimensions: 300x200 pixels or larger
    - The image is automatically resized to 1200x675 and converted to WebP

  PREVIEW VIDEO UPLOAD:
    - Drag and drop a video, or click to browse
    - Supported formats: MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, OGG
    - Maximum size: 100MB (validation) / 500MB (server limit)
    - This video plays on hover over the course card in the catalog

------------------------------------------------------------
5C. ADDING LESSONS TO A COURSE
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: From the course edit page, click "Manage Lessons" or go to
          /instructor/courses/{courseId}/lessons

  Step 2: The page shows a table of existing lessons (left) and the
          "Add Lesson" form (right sidebar)

  Step 3: Fill in the lesson form:

    REQUIRED fields:
      * Lesson Title     - The name of this lesson
      * At least ONE media source (choose one or more):
        - Video URL: A YouTube, Vimeo, or direct video link
        - Video File: Upload a video (MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, max 500MB)
        - Document File: Upload a document (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX,
          XLS, XLSX, max 50MB)

    OPTIONAL fields:
      - Lesson Description / Content - Brief overview of the lesson
      - Duration                     - e.g. "15 min"
      - Status                       - Published (default) or Draft
      - Free Preview checkbox        - Allow viewing without enrollment

  Step 4: Click "Add Lesson"

  What happens:
    - The lesson is added to the course
    - Lessons are automatically ordered (you can also reorder via AJAX)
    - You are redirected back to the lesson list

  You can add as many lessons as needed. Repeat for each lesson.

  MEDIA RULES:
    - You MUST provide at least one media source (URL, video, or document)
    - You can provide multiple (e.g. both a video URL and a document)
    - Video files: max 500MB
    - Document files: max 50MB
    - If an upload fails, the system logs the error gracefully

  After adding lessons, you can use the "Course Tools" section at the
  bottom of the page to:
  - Manage Quizzes for this course
  - Manage Assignments for this course
  - Preview the course as a student would see it

------------------------------------------------------------
5D. CREATING QUIZZES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: Go to /instructor/quiz and click "+ Add Quiz / Exam"
          (or navigate from the course lesson page)

  Step 2: Select quiz type:
    - Quiz (regular practice assessment)
    - Exam (formal graded assessment)

  Step 3: Fill in the quiz form:

    REQUIRED fields:
      * Quiz/Exam Type   - Quiz or Exam (radio button)
      * Title            - e.g. "Chapter 5 Assessment" or "Final Examination"
      * Passing Score    - Percentage needed to pass (0-100, default: 50)
      * Status           - Draft or Published

    OPTIONAL fields:
      - Instructions         - Text instructions for students
      - Instructions File    - Upload PDF, DOC, DOCX, or TXT (max 10MB)
      - Time Limit           - In minutes (leave empty for no time limit)
      - Attempts Limit       - Max number of attempts (leave empty for unlimited)
      - Available From       - Schedule when the quiz becomes available

  Step 4: Click "Create"

  ADDING QUESTIONS:

  After creating the quiz, click "Edit" to add questions.

  For each question:
    * Question Text   - The question students will answer
    * Question Type   - Choose from 8 types:
        1. Multiple Choice    - One correct answer from options
        2. Multiple Select    - Multiple correct answers from options
        3. True/False         - Simple true or false
        4. Short Answer       - Brief text response
        5. Essay              - Long-form response (manual grading)
        6. Fill in the Blank  - Complete the blank
        7. Matching           - Match pairs of items
        8. Ordering           - Put items in correct order
    * Marks           - Points for this question (min: 1)

  Type-specific fields:
    - Multiple Choice / Select: Enter options one per line, specify correct answer
    - True/False: Correct answer is True or False
    - Short Answer: Specify the correct answer text
    - Essay: No correct answer (requires manual grading)
    - Fill in the Blank: Enter correct answers, one per line
    - Matching: Enter key-value pairs (min 2 pairs)
    - Ordering: Enter items in the correct order (min 2 items)

  BULK IMPORT:
    - You can upload a document (PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, max 20MB)
    - The system extracts questions automatically
    - Review extracted questions before saving

  After adding questions, you can:
  - Edit individual questions
  - Delete questions
  - Release results to students (after grading)

  PUBLISHING THE QUIZ:
    - Set status to "Published" to make it visible to enrolled students
    - If you set "Available From", the quiz is scheduled for that date/time
    - Students receive a notification when a quiz is published

------------------------------------------------------------
5E. CREATING ASSIGNMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: Go to /instructor/assignments and click "+ Add Assignment"
          (or navigate from the course lesson page)

  Step 2: Select a course from the dropdown

  Step 3: Fill in the assignment form:

    REQUIRED fields:
      * Title           - The assignment name
      * Description     - Detailed description of the assignment
      * Total Marks     - Maximum score (default: 100)
      * Status          - Draft or Published

    OPTIONAL fields:
      - Instructions         - Additional instructions text
      - Instructions File    - Upload PDF, DOC, DOCX, or TXT (max 10MB)
      - Due Date             - Deadline for submission
      - Available From       - Schedule when the assignment becomes visible

  Step 4: Click "Create Assignment"

  ADVANCED SETTINGS (available in Edit mode):
    - Time Limit (minutes)         - How long students have to complete
    - Max File Size (MB)           - Limit uploaded file sizes (1-50MB)
    - Allowed File Types           - Restrict file types
    - Allow Late Submissions       - Whether to accept past-deadline work
    - Late Penalty Percent         - Deduction for late submissions (0-100%)

  After creation:
    - If status is "Published", enrolled students receive a notification
    - If "Available From" is set, students are notified on that date

  GRADING SUBMISSIONS:
    1. Go to the assignment edit page
    2. View submitted assignments from students
    3. Download their uploaded files
    4. Enter a numeric score (0 to total_marks)
    5. Write feedback
    6. Click "Grade"
    7. The student receives a notification with their grade

------------------------------------------------------------
5F. CREATING COURSE NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Step 1: Click "Course Notes" in the sidebar at /instructor/course-notes
  Step 2: Click "+ Add New Note"

  Step 3: Fill in the note form:

    REQUIRED fields:
      * Course            - Select one of your courses
      * Note Title        - The title of the note
      * Rich Content      - The main content/body of the note (full text)
      * Status            - Draft or Published

    OPTIONAL fields:
      - Description/Summary  - A brief summary (max 1000 characters)
      - Display Order        - Number for ordering (default: 1)
      - Created Date         - Custom date (default: today)
      - Attachment           - Upload any file (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX,
        XLS, XLSX, ZIP, JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP - max 20MB)
      - External Resource Link - URL to an external resource
      - Allow Downloads      - Yes or No (whether students can download)

  Step 4: Click "Save Note"

  Students see published notes in their Course Notes section.

------------------------------------------------------------
5G. MANAGING YOUR COURSES
------------------------------------------------------------

  All Courses: /instructor/courses
    - View all your courses in a table
    - Click "Edit" to modify course details
    - Click "Lessons" to manage lessons for that course

  Edit Course: /instructor/courses/edit/{id}
    - Update any course field
    - Replace thumbnail or preview video
    - Change pricing, status, categories, tags
    - Click "Update Course" to save

  Delete Course: From the course list, click "Delete" with confirmation

  Lesson Reordering:
    - On the lessons page, drag and drop lessons to reorder
    - Changes are saved automatically via AJAX

  Delete Lesson:
    - On the lessons page, click "Delete" next to any lesson

------------------------------------------------------------
5H. INSTRUCTOR FINANCIAL FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------

  Earnings: /instructor/earnings
    - See total earnings, this month's earnings, and pending amount

  Pending Payments: /instructor/pending-payments
    - View offline payment enrollments (Airtel/MTN mobile money)
    - For each pending payment, you can:
      - Click "Approve" to confirm the payment and activate enrollment
      - Click "Reject" to decline the payment
    - Students are notified of your decision

  Payouts: /instructor/payouts
    - See total earnings, total paid, and pending balance

8. COURSE LIFECYCLE & STATUS EXPLANATION
________________________________________________________________________________

Courses go through different statuses:

  DRAFT:
    - The course is hidden from the public catalog
    - Only the instructor/owner can see it
    - Use this status while building your course content
    - Default status when creating a new course

  PENDING:
    - The course is visible to admins for review
    - Useful if you want admin approval before publishing
    - NOT automatically published after admin review

  ACTIVE:
    - The course is live and visible in the public catalog
    - Students can browse, enroll in, and purchase it
    - Use this status when your course is ready for students

  INSTRUCTOR WORKFLOW:
    1. Create course in DRAFT status
    2. Add lessons, quizzes, assignments
    3. Upload thumbnail and preview video
    4. Change status to ACTIVE when ready
    5. Course appears in the catalog for students

  NOTE: Unlike some LMS platforms, EduLab does NOT require admin approval
  for course publishing. Instructors control their own course status.
  Admins can VIEW all courses but cannot change their status.

9. PAYMENT SYSTEM & ENROLLMENT FLOW
________________________________________________________________________________

  FREE COURSES:
    - Students enroll directly with one click
    - No payment required
    - Instant access to all lessons

  PAID COURSES (Paystack Online Payment):
    - Student adds course to cart
    - Proceeds to checkout
    - Enters card details on Paystack's secure page
    - Payment is verified via webhook + callback
    - Enrollment is created automatically
    - Student gets instant access

  PAID COURSES (Offline / Mobile Money):
    - Student adds course to cart
    - Selects offline payment (Airtel or MTN)
    - Places the order
    - Enrollment is created with PENDING payment status
    - Instructor/admin must approve the payment
    - Student is notified once approved

  COUPONS:
    - Admin creates coupons with code, discount type (% or fixed),
      max uses, min amount, and expiration date
    - Students apply coupon codes at checkout
    - Discount is applied to the subtotal before tax

  BUNDLES:
    - Admin creates course bundles (multiple courses at a combined price)
    - Students can purchase bundles to get access to all included courses
    - Enrollment is created for each course in the bundle

  TAX:
    - Configurable tax rate applied at checkout
    - Calculated on (subtotal - coupon discount)

10. ASSESSMENT SYSTEM (QUIZZES, EXAMS & ASSIGNMENTS)
________________________________________________________________________________

  QUIZZES vs EXAMS:
    - Quizzes: Regular practice assessments (is_exam = false)
    - Exams: Formal graded assessments (is_exam = true)
    - Both use the same question types and grading system
    - Exams appear under "Exams" in the student sidebar

  QUESTION TYPES (8 types):
    1. Multiple Choice    - Select one correct answer
    2. Multiple Select    - Select multiple correct answers
    3. True/False         - True or False
    4. Short Answer       - Brief text (auto-graded, case-insensitive)
    5. Essay              - Long text (requires manual instructor grading)
    6. Fill in the Blank  - Complete the missing text
    7. Matching           - Match pairs of items
    8. Ordering           - Arrange items in correct order

  AUTO-GRADING:
    - All types except Essay are auto-graded instantly
    - QuizEnhancementService compares answers against correct answers
    - Score is calculated as percentage
    - Pass/fail determined by passing_score threshold

  TIME LIMITS:
    - Instructor sets time limit in minutes (optional)
    - Timer starts when student begins the quiz
    - If time expires, quiz auto-submits with current answers
    - Server-side enforcement prevents cheating

  ATTEMPT LIMITS:
    - Instructor can limit the number of attempts
    - System tracks attempts per student per quiz

  ASSIGNMENTS:
    - Manually graded by the instructor
    - Students can submit text and/or file uploads
    - Deadline enforcement: late submissions may be rejected or penalized
    - Instructor sets penalty percentage for late work
    - Max file size and allowed file types are configurable

  RESULT RELEASE:
    - Instructor can control when quiz results are visible to students
    - "Release Results" button sends all results at once
    - Until released, students see "Awaiting Review"

19. TROUBLESHOOTING FAQ
________________________________________________________________________________

Q: I registered as an instructor but cannot create courses.
A: You need admin approval. Check your notifications for approval status.
   Contact the platform administrator if you have been waiting too long.

Q: I tried to log in and got "This account is registered as Student,
   not Instructor."
A: You selected the wrong role tab on the login page. Click the correct
   role tab (Student, Instructor, or Organization) that matches your
   registration.

Q: I uploaded a course but students cannot see it.
A: Check your course status. It must be "Active" to appear in the catalog.
   "Draft" courses are hidden. Change it to Active via the course edit page.

Q: Students say they cannot see the "Add Assignment" or "Add Quiz" button.
A: Make sure the course is in Active status and you are viewing the correct
   page. The buttons appear on the course-specific quiz/assignment pages,
   not the overview pages.

Q: My uploaded video is not playing.
A: Check the file format (MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, OGG) and size (max 100MB
   for preview, 500MB for lessons). Ensure the server has sufficient
   storage space.

Q: I cannot submit an assignment.
A: Check the due date. If the deadline has passed and late submissions are
   not allowed, you cannot submit. Also ensure you are providing at least
   one of: text content or a file upload.

Q: My certificate was not generated after completing all lessons.
A: Ensure you have marked ALL lessons as complete. The system auto-generates
   the certificate when the last lesson is completed. If it still fails,
   contact admin to create one manually at /admin/certificate/create.

Q: The "Pending Approval" page won't go away.
A: An admin needs to approve your instructor account. Go to /admin/settings
   (as admin), click the "Instructor Approval" tab, and approve the
   instructor.

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Go to Settings in your sidebar. Enter your current password and new
   password. The new password must be at least 8 characters with letters,
   mixed case, numbers, and symbols.

Q: I cannot see the course notes from my instructor.
A: Ensure you are enrolled in the course AND the note is published.
   Only published notes are visible to students.
